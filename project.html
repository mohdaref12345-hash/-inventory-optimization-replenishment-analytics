04 / Methodology

Inventory calculations

The report uses standard inventory formulas to present replenishment and cost-related measures.

Reorder Point (ROP)

ROP = Average Daily Demand × Lead Time + Safety Stock

Lead Time Demand

Lead Time Demand = Average Daily Demand × Lead Time (Days)

Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)

EOQ = √((2 × Annual Demand × Ordering Cost per Order) / Annual Holding Cost per Unit)

Annual Holding Cost

Annual Holding Cost = Current Stock × Annual Holding Cost per Unit

Annual Ordering Cost

Annual Ordering Cost = (Annual Demand / EOQ) × Ordering Cost per Order

Assumptions: The ordering cost per order and annual holding cost per unit used in the optimization analysis are assumed input values for analytical purposes, not verified actual company costs. The project is a decision-support report, not a fully optimized procurement model or an automated inventory management system.
